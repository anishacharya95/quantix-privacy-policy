Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Quantix Android application handles, why it is used, and which third-party services may process it.

1. About Quantix

Quantix is a market-information and analysis application covering Nepal (NEPSE), India (NSE/BSE), United States markets and supported cryptocurrency spot markets. Features include market quotes, charts, security search and screening, watchlists, locally stored portfolio entries, price alerts, financial-news links, technical indicators and educational analytics.

Quantix is an information and analysis tool. It does not execute trades, hold funds, provide brokerage services or provide personalised investment advice. Market information may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable.

2. Information stored on your device

Quantix stores certain information locally on your device so app features can work. Depending on what you use, this may include:

  • Watchlist selections and saved market symbols.
  • Portfolio holdings, quantities, cost basis and other values you enter.
  • Price-alert symbols, thresholds and preferences.
  • App appearance preferences and cached market responses.
  • The optional SEC contact string you enter in Settings for identification in relevant SEC data requests.

These items are stored in the app's local storage. The current app does not provide cloud synchronization for your watchlist, portfolio, alerts or SEC contact setting. Quantix does not send your locally entered portfolio holdings or watchlist to a Quantix account database. Market-data requests may, however, send the symbol or market you request to the relevant data provider.

You can clear cached information using the app's cache control where available, or clear the app's storage/uninstall it to remove locally stored app data. Android backup or device-transfer features may separately handle app data according to your device and Google account settings.

3. Information sent to market-data and content providers

When you request quotes, charts, company information, filings or news, Quantix sends requests to external services. A request may contain the market, ticker or cryptocurrency pair, chart range or other query you selected, together with ordinary network information such as your IP address, request time and technical request details. The provider receiving the request may process and retain that information under its own policies.

The current app uses the following data and content sources:

  • Yahoo Finance: quotes, historical chart data and financial-news RSS.
  • Nasdaq public quote API: fallback quote information for supported US securities.
  • NSE and BSE public market information: Indian securities directories and BSE daily bhavcopy/end-of-day information.
  • Binance public market-data API: supported spot-market listings, tickers and candles.
  • NEPSE Pulse service: Nepal market and security information through its API.
  • SEC EDGAR: US company facts, filings and submissions. If you enter an SEC contact string in Settings, it may be included in relevant SEC requests.
  • Google News RSS: financial-news headlines and source links.
  • Tracefour Congress API: public US congressional-disclosure information and source links.

These services are operated independently of Quantix. Their availability, security, retention and privacy practices are governed by their own terms and policies. Quantix does not control how they process information after receiving a request.

4. Accounts and authentication

Quantix requires sign-in and uses Google Firebase Authentication. You may register or sign in using an email address and password, and Google Sign-In may be available when the required Google configuration is enabled.

For authentication, Firebase/Google may process your email address, Firebase user identifier, authentication-provider information, account status and sign-in/session information. When you register, the name you provide may be saved as your Firebase profile display name. If you use Google Sign-In, Google may provide basic profile information such as your name and email address. Firebase also handles email verification and password-reset messages.

Your password is submitted to Firebase Authentication for authentication; Quantix does not store your password in its own app database. Authentication data is processed by Google under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. Quantix does not currently use Firebase to synchronize your watchlist, portfolio or alerts.

You can sign out from the app. For questions about an account or to request account-related assistance, contact us using the email in Section 12. Google may retain authentication records according to its own policies and applicable law.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Quantix uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google AdMob to display advertising. The current app includes a banner-ad placement. The SDK and Google may collect or receive information such as your device advertising identifier (where available), IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad requests, ad views, interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals. Google uses this information to deliver, measure, limit and help protect the advertising service.

Whether an ad is personalized may depend on your device and Google account settings, applicable regional requirements and Google's ad-serving configuration. You can review advertising personalization and reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your Android or Google settings where those controls are available.

Google and its advertising partners process advertising information under their own policies. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how AdMob uses data. Quantix does not sell your personal information.

6. Permissions, analytics and device information

The Android app requests internet access to retrieve market information, authenticate accounts and load advertisements. The current Android manifest does not request device location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Quantix does not need your GPS location to provide its market-information features.

The current app code does not include a separate Quantix analytics SDK. Firebase Authentication and Google AdMob may still process technical, diagnostic, security and service-use information as described in this policy and in their own policies.

7. How information is used

Information handled by Quantix and its service providers is used to:

  • Create and authenticate your account, maintain sign-in and verify your email address.
  • Retrieve the market data, charts, company information, filings and news you request.
  • Save your watchlist, portfolio entries, alerts and preferences on your device.
  • Evaluate on-device price alerts and display analysis based on market data.
  • Cache responses, maintain app functionality, diagnose errors and protect services.
  • Request, display, measure and help prevent fraud in advertisements.

8. Data retention and deletion

Watchlists, portfolio entries, alerts, preferences and cached responses remain in local app storage until you change or clear them, the app removes expired cache entries, or you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The current app does not maintain a Quantix cloud database for synchronizing those items.

Firebase Authentication account information is handled by Google/Firebase and may be retained under Google's policies. To request assistance with account deletion or other account-related privacy matters, contact us at the email in Section 12. Information sent to independent market-data, news or advertising providers is subject to those providers' retention practices; requests about their records should be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Sharing and sale of information

Quantix does not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed for the functions described in this policy, including Firebase Authentication, Google AdMob and the market-data/content services listed above. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

10. Children's privacy

Quantix is a general financial-information tool and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with Quantix, contact us so we can review the request.

11. Security and changes to this policy

Quantix uses app-local storage and HTTPS-based network connections where supported by its configured services. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Keep your device protected and do not enter confidential financial credentials into the app.

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page. The effective date at the top indicates when the current version took effect.

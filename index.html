Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quantix handles information when you use the Quantix Android application.

1. About Quantix

Quantix is a market-information and analysis application covering Nepal (NEPSE), India (NSE/BSE), United States markets and supported cryptocurrency spot markets. It provides market quotes, charts, screening tools, watchlists, locally stored portfolio entries, price alerts, news links and educational analytics.

Quantix is an information tool. It does not execute trades, hold funds, provide brokerage services or provide personalised investment advice.

2. Information stored on your device

Quantix stores certain app information locally on your device so its features can work. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Watchlist selections and saved market symbols.
  • Portfolio holdings and values that you enter into the app.
  • Price-alert settings that you create.
  • App preferences and cached market responses.
  • A contact string you optionally enter in Settings for SEC data-request identification. The app documentation indicates this setting is stored on your device.

These features are designed for on-device use; the app does not provide a Quantix account or cloud-sync service for this information. Cached market data may be removed using the app's cache controls where available, or by clearing the app's data/uninstalling the app.

Android's own backup, device-transfer and system features may handle app data according to your device and Google account settings. Quantix does not control those operating-system services.

3. Information sent to third-party services

To retrieve market information, charts, company information and headlines, Quantix connects to external data providers and services. Requests may include the market, ticker or cryptocurrency pair you search for, requested chart period, and ordinary technical information required for an internet request (such as your IP address, request time and app/network details).

The providers documented in the current app include:

  • Yahoo Finance — market quotes, historical chart data and financial-news RSS.
  • Nasdaq public quote API — fallback quote information for supported US securities.
  • NSE and BSE public market information — Indian securities directories and BSE daily bhavcopy information.
  • Binance public market-data API — supported spot-market listings, tickers and candles.
  • NEPSE Pulse service — Nepal market and security information through its public API.
  • SEC EDGAR — US company facts, filings and related public information. If you enter an SEC contact string in Settings, it is used in relevant SEC requests.
  • Google News RSS — financial-news headlines and source links.
  • Tracefour Congress API — public congressional-disclosure information and source links.

These services operate independently and process requests under their own privacy policies and terms. Quantix does not control their data practices, availability or retention. Please review the applicable provider policies if you want details about their handling of network requests.

4. Accounts, advertising and tracking

The current app does not require you to create a Quantix account. The reviewed project configuration includes internet access for retrieving data; it does not declare permissions for contacts, location, camera or microphone. The reviewed app configuration also does not show an integrated advertising or analytics SDK.

We do not use the app to sell your personal information. This statement describes the current app implementation and may be updated if future versions add accounts, advertising, analytics or other data-processing features.

5. How information is used

Information handled by Quantix is used to provide the features you request, including loading market data and news, displaying charts and analytics, maintaining your on-device watchlist and portfolio, evaluating local price alerts, caching responses and troubleshooting failures.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information saved by the app remains in its local app storage until you change or clear it, the app removes expired cache entries, or you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Quantix does not operate a cloud account database for syncing your watchlist, portfolio or alerts in the current version.

Information sent to external providers is subject to those providers' retention practices. Quantix cannot delete information held independently by those providers; requests about their records should be directed to the relevant provider.

7. Children's privacy

Quantix is a general financial-information tool and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us using the details below.

8. Security

Quantix uses app-local storage and HTTPS-based network connections where supported by its configured services. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device protected and avoid entering confidential information into the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page. The effective date at the top indicates when this version was last updated.
